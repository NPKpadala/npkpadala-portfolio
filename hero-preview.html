What this is

A staging page for the From Idea to Orbit hero — eight acts on a loop, built with React and Framer Motion, rendered entirely as SVG. It lives here rather than on the homepage until the placement is decided, so nothing on the live hero changes in the meantime.

Source and the reasoning behind each decision are in hero/ — see hero/README.md.

Things worth trying: scroll down and back (the animation stops while it is off screen), switch tabs (it stops there too), move the pointer around (three starfield layers and the stage drift by different amounts), hit Pause, and turn on Reduce motion in your OS — that last one swaps the animation for a static poster of the whole pipeline rather than freezing on one act.
